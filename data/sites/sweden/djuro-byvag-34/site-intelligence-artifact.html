Why DERIVED, not AUTHORITATIVE

Source pixels are official Lantmäteriet 1 m grid data (real, receipted, SHA-256 verified) — but slope and aspect are a computation on top of that raster, not an official surveyed value. Sub-metre breaklines and any post-2026 site regrading are not captured.
