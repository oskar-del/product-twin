### Reading the matrix
- **A** and **F** are strong but each carries one structural flaw: A shades its own evening terrace and leans on an unmeasured slope; F pays a cost/buildability tax for angled structure.
- **B** and **D** score worst on the axes that matter most here (view/economics for B, resale/thermal for D) — confirming they should be *mined for grafts*, not built.
- **E Vinkelhuset mot Glan** leads because it is the only option that scores ≥4 on *every* weighted-heavy axis simultaneously: it takes the south view and winter sun like the bar (A), earns evening sun and wind shelter like the court (B's heart), shortens the heated envelope like the split (C), and stays orthogonal and slope-agnostic — the cheap, resale-safe, low-risk answer that still gives one clear gesture.

## Recommendation → **E · "Vinkelhuset mot Glan"**
An L-plan longhouse: south bar for lake view + winter sun, a cold garage/service wing stepping north off the east end, the west side left open to WNW evening sun, and a built glazed windbreak turning the SW terrace corner into a sheltered *kvällssol* suntrap. Developed in sheet 04.

### Graftable ideas carried from the losers
- **From B (Gårdshuset):** the sheltered *heart* — realised as the **three-sided SW terrace pocket + vindficka windbreak**, open to view, not a closed court.
- **From C (Två Volymer):** the **garage as a linked cold volume** — becomes the north wing that also forms the entry court and screens NE/E wind, keeping the heated envelope compact.
- **From D (Paviljongen):** one **small detached sauna/guest pod** by the water as an optional phase-2 asset (not the house).
- **From F (Solfjädern):** **splay the glazing (not the structure)** of the west view-room — the signature gesture at near-zero buildability cost.
- **From A (Långhuset):** the efficient **south bar spine** itself, and the **walk-out souterräng** held as a *conditional upgrade* on the west end — activated only if MIMER's 1 m DTM confirms a south fall ≳2.5 m.

## Decision note (near scope boundary → flag to Brain)
This study commits BRAGE to design geometry (walls, House Heart) on a plot whose **legal boundary, buildable envelope (BYA/BTA), access and terrain are all still open gates.** Every element is labelled `CONCEPT` accordingly. Two things should be requested before v0.3: (1) the **1 m DTM** (unlocks slope → confirm/deny the souterräng upgrade and set finished-floor), and (2) a **viewshed** from the proposed south glass at eye height (upgrades the Glan view from REPORTED to DERIVED — and becomes a listing "View Certificate"). Both are MIMER/Platform requests above BRAGE's scope.
