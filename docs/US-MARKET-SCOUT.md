# US market scout — plot-to-project entry assessment
Researched 2026-08-19 (web-sourced, citations in research transcript). Status: SCOUTING ONLY — US stays parked in Expansion brain until an explicit unpark decision.

## Data landscape (vs Lantmäteriet/Catastro)
No national cadastre — parcels live with ~3,100 counties. BUT the federal half is world-class and FREE: USGS 3DEP lidar (99% national coverage, 1m DEM + point clouds — better than Sweden in places), FEMA flood layers, USDA SSURGO soils (incl. septic suitability — feeds our systems screen directly). Parcels: free in open states (FL statewide 10.8M, NY, MT, NC; TX county-by-county) or Regrid at $10/mo retail / ~$80k/yr bulk. THE WEAK LAYER = zoning: ~33,000 jurisdictions, no standard, every aggregator partial. MLS/listings: fragmented (~500 MLSs), land often not on MLS at all — out of scope v1.

## Competitive gap — confirmed
Data vendors (Regrid=supplier not competitor), pretty-map tools (Land id $399–800/yr — closest, proves agents PAY for land presentation; has 3D tours but no verdicts), urban feasibility (Deepblocks $18–5,999/mo, TestFit, Giraffe — no terrain/rural/evidence), flipper lead-gen (PropStream $99/mo — finds sellers, doesn't evaluate land). NOBODY does evidence-receipted dossier + viewshed + buildable envelope + systems/BoM + 3D twin.

## Verdict
**Wedge if ever entered: "view-verified land dossiers" for rural-land agents/sellers/buyers, starting TEXAS HILL COUNTRY** — counties can't zone unincorporated land (weakest layer disappears by jurisdiction choice), views drive rural price, remaining screens (OSSF septic + FEMA flood) are free data. 2-week proof spec: 2–3 counties, ~25 real LandWatch listings → auto-dossiers + twin microsites, ship 5 public, cold-email the 5 listing agents (Opero audit playbook). Success = 2 replies or 1 paid pilot.

## Why NOT now (agent's honest top 3)
1. Zero US presence/network — our whole playbook runs on warm nodes and operational proximity; Land id/onX own this audience's attention.
2. Zoning quicksand the moment the wedge widens beyond unzoned-rural.
3. Focus cost: a 4th market, 4th data stack, 4th legal context, no shared graph with Munin — while ES corridor + UK + Nancy compound existing moats.
**Trigger to unpark: a US-side distribution partner (agent/brokerage) materializes → run the 2-week proof, it's cheap.**
