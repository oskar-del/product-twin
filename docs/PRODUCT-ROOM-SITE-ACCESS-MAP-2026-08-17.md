## Site, climate and garden state

### Verified context

- Project location context: Marbella, Málaga, postcode 29660; screening coordinate 36.51, -4.97; elevation 52 m.
- CTE climate zone: A3, verified against DB-HE Annex B.
- PVGIS-SARAH3 site baseline: 1,732.34 kWh/kWp/year, 2,154.05 kWh/m² annual plane irradiation.
- PVGIS optimized screening plane: 33° tilt and 2° west of south in the PVGIS convention.
- PVGIS horizon calculation was enabled.
- The showroom direction already includes framed garden views, pool terrace, outdoor sofa/dining, shade, water and planting.

### Not yet available

- Legal/survey plot boundary and coordinates.
- Terrain/topography, levels and retaining conditions.
- Building footprint, openings, roof and obstruction geometry for a true sun/shadow study.
- Existing trees and protected vegetation.
- Soil texture, drainage, pH/salinity and contamination.
- Irrigation source, water budget and local restrictions.
- Wind exposure, salt exposure and garden microclimates.
- Desired maintenance level, pets/children, allergies, edible planting and seasonal occupancy.
- A plant Product Twin taxonomy with mature size, bloom season, water need, sun/shade, toxicity and sourcing.

The garden is therefore **ready to scope**, not ready for a site-specific planting prescription. We can still build a climate-aware Marbella demo garden once assumptions are explicitly labeled.

## E-commerce / SEO extension

The room experience can sit on top of a conventional catalogue rather than replacing it:

- Product Twin pages: identity, dimensions, verified evidence, avatar, fit rules, alternatives and offer handoff.
- Room pages: complete shoppable arrangements with a style story and total budget.
- Style collections: Marbella Warm Modern, Nordic Mediterranean, Resort at Home and similar curated worlds.
- Comparison pages: same-size, lower-cost, local, lower-carbon or faster-delivery substitutes.
- Garden collections: sun exposure, water budget, bloom season, mature form and outdoor-room compatibility.

Potential revenue lanes are affiliate commission, qualified supplier/RFQ leads, managed procurement, design services and manufacturer catalogue/analytics services. Before public monetization, each merchant programme, product-image licence, geometry right, price/stock refresh policy and affiliate disclosure must be verified. SEO pages must provide real design/fit/evidence value rather than thin catalogue duplication.
