Researched 2026-08-19 (web-sourced, citations inline). Companion to VALUE-STORY-AND-IDEA-LEDGER.md. Status: RESEARCH — no bank has been approached.

## Sweden — byggnadskreditiv document stack
Per Konsumenternas + bank product pages (Handelsbanken, SEB, Swedbank, Danske, Nordea): produktionskostnadskalkyl · tomtkarta + köpekontrakt · entreprenadkontrakt (ABS 18) / bindande offerter · bygglov + startbesked · teknisk dokumentation · lyftplan (drawdown vs stages). Statutory gates: färdigställandeskydd (Lag 2014:227 — startbesked blocked without it), kontrollansvarig + kontrollplan (PBL), BBR energiberäkning (verified per BEN). Bank practice: 10–20% own funds, credit ≤ ~85–90% of finished value, dispositionsränta + fees, invoice-based drawdowns with bank inspector/valuation. Danske = existing customers only. Internal credit thresholds NOT public at any bank.

## Spain — hipoteca autopromotor stack
Nota simple (clean tenure) · proyecto de ejecución visado (colegio) · licencia de obras · presupuesto/contrato · tasación under Orden ECO/805/2003 "hipótesis de edificio terminado" (legally requires visado + licencia + CEE registration) · certificaciones de obra unlock tranches · ~20–30% own funds. Statutory: seguro decenal (LOE) — exempt for own-use autopromotor BUT resale within 10 years triggers it (registrar-enforced); OCT audit ~0.2–0.4% of PEM must run FROM FOUNDATIONS (unretrofittable); off-plan advances need aval/caución + cuenta especial (Ley 20/2015). Licencia = choke point: statutory 3 months, real-world up to 1 year+; top causes: incomplete docs, CTE deficiencies, missing CEE, cadastral discrepancies.

## Capability map
AUTO (our pipeline): parcel/tenure evidence w receipts · catastro↔registro discrepancy check (ES) · terrain/geotech/flood/viewshed risk screen (banks currently BLIND here) · planning-vs-design pre-check · independent BoM cost w live dated prices · quantity-based lyftplan/drawdown schedule · finished-value comps (incl. penotariado layer ES) · licencia-readiness score (ES).
ATTACH-ONLY (licensed): stamped structural calcs, proyecto visado, tasación ECO, KA/kontrollplan, OCT, insurance policies — we verify stamp/validity + pre-assemble their evidence file, never author.
Every dossier ends with a mandatory "NOT verified / assumptions" ledger.

## The five sales wedges
1. **The core credit document is authored by the counterparty** — kostnadskalkyl comes from the husleverantör; presupuesto from the constructor. Our receipted BoM = "the only cost number in the file not written by someone paid from the loan."
2. **Banks see zero ground-risk data** — no geotech/terrain/flood in any bank's stack; grundläggning is the classic overrun. Pure additive value, no incumbent.
3. **Cost-vs-finished-value gap** = the bank's #1 stated fear, today a one-shot valuation; our comps quantify it continuously.
4. **Drawdown control is invoice-trust, not quantity-trust** — nobody reconciles invoices against a quantity BoM ("does invoice 14 match 38 m³ concrete at today's price?").
5. **Tasadoras as channel, not competitor** — ECO/805/2003 hard-codes their prerequisites; pre-assembling that file makes Tinsa/ST/Arquitasa distributors of our dossier.
