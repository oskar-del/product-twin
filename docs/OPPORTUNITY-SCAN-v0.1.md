## THE INSIGHT
The 4 winners are ALL PROPERTY-ADJACENT: solar (the roof), mortgages (financing the purchase), insurance (the home/life), wills/conveyancing (the transaction). We don't need a new vertical — we EXTEND the property funnel. A buyer/owner in our ecosystem needs a mortgage, home insurance, solar, and eventually a will. Same audience, same data spine, each a new affiliate rail on traffic we already generate.
