## Evidence separation

The product keeps these categories separate:

- municipal locator and indicative map surface;
- official screening and recorded law/plan sources;
- market statements;
- visual reconstructions; and
- verified design geometry.

No house, room, buildable envelope, legal access point, registered boundary or surveyed terrain is displayed as established truth.
