# For manufacturers: your product, placed where people are actually buying homes

Most 3D/BIM programs end at a download. A file leaves your server and you never learn whether it sold anything. We close that loop.

## What we do

We build a **verified twin** of your exact product — real identity (SKU/EAN), manufacturer dimensions, your actual materials and finish, checked against your own geometry, not a look-alike — and place it inside **real, for-sale properties** that buyers are browsing right now. When someone furnishes the home they're about to buy, they're furnishing it with your product, linked straight back to your official offer or RFQ route.

## What you get back

- **Placement in live purchase intent.** Not a catalogue page — a specific product, in a specific room, in a home a specific person is deciding to buy.
- **Choice and swap analytics.** How often your piece is picked, what it's picked *over*, what it's placed *with*, where it's swapped out and for what. The demand signal your download portal never gave you.
- **A qualified route to the buyer.** Every placement carries a clean line back to your commercial channel — no resale, no sublicensing, no scraping of your price or stock. Live price and availability are always pulled from your own route.

## What it costs you

Authorization to use one exact asset. That's the whole ask. No fee, no integration project, no data you have to send us. You keep your IP, your identity, your pricing channel. We display your product *as your product* and never claim otherwise.

## Why this is credible, not a pitch

The machine is already running on other products:

- Exact-identity twins with **manufacturer-verified dimensions** and disclosed materials, placed in real property scenes.
- The **purchase path is already proven** end to end on comparable products — the missing piece for any given SKU is authoritative delivery to the buyer's location, which we resolve through your own channel.
- Strict provenance: a twin is never promoted to "exact product" until its identity, scale, materials and your usage rights are each independently verified. We would rather show a product as a disclosed proxy than misrepresent it as yours.

## What we ask of you

1. One authorized exact asset (or professional-account access to your existing 3D/BIM files).
2. Written scope for rendering and display — what's in, what's out. You draw the line; we stay inside it.
3. Your official delivery/RFQ contact for the markets we place in.

You give an asset and a permission. You get your product in front of people at the one moment they're deciding how to fill a home — and, for the first time, the data on whether they chose it.
